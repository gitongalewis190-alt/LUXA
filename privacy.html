Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This is a template policy provided for informational purposes during LUXA's early development. It has not been drafted or reviewed by a licensed attorney. Have it reviewed by legal counsel before relying on it for compliance or operating the platform with real users.

1 Information We Collect

When you create a LUXA account or use the platform, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information: name, email address, password (stored in hashed form by our authentication provider), and profile details you choose to add.
  • Vehicle & listing data: information you submit about vehicles you list, browse, save, or inquire about.
  • Usage data: pages visited, features used, search queries, and interactions within the dashboard, community, and shop areas of the platform.
  • Device & technical data: browser type, IP address, and similar diagnostic information collected automatically.
  • Communications: messages you send to support, community posts, and any information you voluntarily submit through forms (such as career-alert email signups).

2 How We Use It

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the LUXA platform and its features (dashboard, garage, shop, services, community, and support).
  • Authenticate your account and keep it secure.
  • Respond to support requests and communicate important account or service updates.
  • Improve and personalize the platform based on how it's used.
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, or violations of our Terms of Service.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

3 Cookies

LUXA uses cookies and similar local-storage technologies to keep you signed in, remember your preferences (such as saved vehicles or dashboard settings), and understand how the platform is used. Some features described elsewhere on the platform — such as career-notification signups — store data directly in your browser's local storage rather than on a server.

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings, though disabling them may limit certain features of the platform.

4 Third-Party Services

LUXA relies on trusted third-party services to operate the platform, including:

  • Firebase Authentication (provided by Google) — used to manage account sign-up, sign-in, and session security. Firebase processes the credentials and identifiers necessary to authenticate your account in accordance with its own privacy and security practices.
  • Other infrastructure, analytics, or payment providers we may adopt as the platform grows, which will be disclosed here as they are integrated.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

5 Data Retention

We retain account and usage information for as long as your account remains active, or as needed to provide the platform's services. If you delete your account, we will remove or anonymize your personal information within a reasonable period, except where retention is required to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or enforce our agreements.

6 Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated personal data.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing of your information.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

7 Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, reach out via our Support page.
